# Sahara Grill Menu — editing guide

## Open the editor

1. Go to **https://www.thesaharagrill.com/studio** (locally: http://localhost:3001/studio).
2. Sign in with the account you were invited to.
3. You’ll see three areas:
   - **Food** — food sections and items
   - **Drinks** — drinks sections and items
   - **Menu Page Settings** — page title, tab labels, intro text, SEO

## Edit a dish or drink

1. Open **Food** or **Drinks**.
2. Open the section (for example, Wraps).
3. Change the name, price, or description.
4. Drag items to reorder them.
5. Use **Show on website** to hide an item temporarily without deleting it.
6. Click **Publish** when you’re ready. Drafts are not public until you publish.

## Edit a section photo

1. Open the section.
2. Upload or replace **Section photo**.
3. Fill in **Photo description (alt text)** (short, clear description).
4. Keep **Photo alignment** as-is unless you intentionally want the layout flipped.
5. Publish.

## Change page title, tabs, or SEO

1. Open **Menu Page Settings**.
2. Update the heading, optional intro, Food/Drinks labels, and SEO fields.
3. Publish.

## What guests see after Publish

- Publishing clears the website's menu cache, usually within a few seconds. Open or refresh the menu page to see the update; already-open browser tabs do not refresh themselves.
- You do **not** need a developer redeploy for normal menu edits.
- If something looks stale, wait about a minute and refresh again. A backup cache refresh becomes eligible after 60 seconds and runs when someone requests the page.

## Tips

- Prefer hiding items/sections over deleting them if they might return.
- Double-check prices before publishing.
- If Studio asks you to log in again, use the same invited email.

## Who should own the Sanity project?

- Prefer the **restaurant / client organization** as the project owner when possible.
- Your agency can stay as an Admin collaborator to help maintain the site.
- Sanity Free currently offers **Administrator** and **Viewer** roles. Viewer cannot edit; Administrator also has project-management access. A restricted **Editor** role requires a paid plan. Decide on the appropriate role before inviting staff; do not upgrade automatically.
- Avoid sharing the owner login; invite people instead.
